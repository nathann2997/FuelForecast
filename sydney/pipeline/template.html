How the forecast works, and how good it is

  • Target. Change in the Sydney median pump price over 1, 3, 7 and 14 days for U91, E10, Premium 98 and diesel, plus the chance of a 5c+ jump within 7 days.
  • Inputs. Pump margin over the terminal gate price (TGP) and how it compares with the past year; wholesale moves over 1 to 28 days; Brent crude in Australian dollars and the AUD; how spread out station prices are and how many stations repriced recently; and cycle memory (recent momentum, distance from the 4-week high and low, days since the last price jump).
  • Model. Gradient-boosted trees (LightGBM, Huber loss) pooled across the four fuels, with a separate classifier for price jumps.
  • Validation. Walk-forward: retrained every month on all data before it, tested on each month from Jan 2021, including the 2022 and 2026 oil shocks. Ranges are 80% intervals from the past year of test errors.
  • Cycle memory needs about four weeks of unbroken daily data. Until the daily refresh has built that up after the September gap, the live forecast uses the model without it, which tested almost as well.
  • Limits. It reacts to oil and wholesale moves as they happen but cannot see wars, OPEC decisions or excise changes coming.

What this page was built from

Sources: NSW Government FuelCheck API (live) and monthly price history on data.nsw.gov.au; Australian Institute of Petroleum terminal gate prices; Brent crude (DCOILBRENTEU) and AUD/USD (DEXUSAL) from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
